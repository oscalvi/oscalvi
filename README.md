# Hi, I'm Oscar Alvarez Villarreal 👋

**Computer engineer · 45 years in technology, from punch cards to AI** · Miami, FL

I started in 1981 as a data-entry clerk at Colombia's national statistics agency (DANE), moved up to mainframe operator and then COBOL/Fortran programmer, maintaining the consumer price index system. Since then I have built accounting and administrative systems, led a seven-developer team integrating Kofax and Documentum at Kemper/Broadspire (Aetna), and run my own consulting firm for almost two decades.

Today I direct AI to build useful, everyday software — with real design underneath.

### What I believe

For decades, business technology was only for companies that could pay for servers, licenses and programming teams. AI changed that. The scarce skill is no longer typing code; it is knowing what to ask for, designing it well, and reviewing what comes back. With good design, an independent professional can now have the tools of a mid-sized company — **a complete office on any entrepreneur's phone.**
